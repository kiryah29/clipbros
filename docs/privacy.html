Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what Clip bros app ("the app", "we") collects and how it is used.

1. What we collect

  • TikTok authorisation data: when you connect TikTok, we receive an access token and refresh token, your TikTok open ID, and basic profile info (display name and avatar) that TikTok shares through Login Kit, limited to the permissions you approve.
  • Videos: the clips you create or approve for posting, and their titles and captions.
  • Stream data: public chat activity from the live channels you choose to monitor, used to detect highlights.

2. How we use it

Only to run the app: to show you your clips, and to upload the videos you approve to your TikTok account through the Content Posting API. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties other than TikTok as needed to post your videos.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and videos are stored on the system where the app runs and are not made public. Access tokens are kept only as long as needed to post for you.

4. Retention and deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok (Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions). After you do, or if you ask us, we delete your stored tokens and related data. Videos you already posted on TikTok stay on TikTok until you delete them there.

5. Children

The app is not for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age TikTok requires.

6. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

7. Contact

For questions or deletion requests, open an issue at github.com/kiryah29/clipsbros/issues.
