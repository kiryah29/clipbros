Clip bros app

Clip bros app helps creators turn the best moments of their own live streams into short vertical clips and publish them to their own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The tool watches the creator's live stream chat and detects spikes in activity.
  2. It cuts a clip around each spike and renders a 9:16 video with captions.
  3. The creator reviews each clip and chooses whether to publish it.
  4. When the creator connects their TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit, the app uses the Content Posting API to send the approved clip to that account.

Videos are only posted to the account of the creator who connected it, and only after that creator approves each video.

Source code: github.com/kiryah29/clipsbros
